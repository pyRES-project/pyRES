4. ECONOMICS MODULE IMPLEMENTATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FILE: src/rec_sim/Economics.py
─────────────────────────────

Calculation framework:
├─ Input: components (PV, BESS), annual_en_flows_and_prices
├─ Output: Financial metrics over investment horizon
├─ Time horizon: Typically 20-25 years
├─ Key parameters applied:
│  ├─ tax_rate: Applied to grid sales revenue (r1)
│  │  └─ c4 = r1 × tax_rate
│  ├─ int_rate: Discount rate for NPV
│  │  └─ NPV = npv(int_rate, cashflow)
│  └─ other_capex_perc: Multiplier for total CAPEX
│     └─ outflow[0] = sum(components CAPEX) / (1 - sum(other_capex_perc))
│
├─ Annual flows:
│  ├─ r1: Revenue from grid electricity sales
│  │  └─ r1 = sold_energy × price_sold × (1-decay)^(year-1)
│  ├─ r2: Savings from self-consumption
│  │  └─ r2 = self_cons_energy × price_buy × (1-decay)^(year-1)
│  ├─ r3: Incentives (from inc_year of components)
│  ├─ c1: Cost of grid electricity purchase
│  │  └─ c1 = purchased_energy × price_buy × (1-decay)^(year-1) [FIX #2]
│  ├─ c2: Annual O&M costs
│  ├─ c3: Annual taxes (from tax_year of components)
│  ├─ c4: Tax on sales revenue
│  │  └─ c4 = r1 × tax_rate
│  ├─ c5: Other costs (from other_cost of components)
│  └─ c6: BESS replacement costs
│     └─ Applied at battery lifetime (e.g., year 15)
│
├─ Key fixes applied:
│  ├─ [FIX #2] Price decay now applied to purchased energy cost (c1)
│  │  └─ Previously: only applied to sales, not purchases
│  ├─ [FIX #3] OPEX and tax NOT zeroed after calculation
│  │  └─ Previously: zeroed after investment cost calculation, causing wrong totals
│  ├─ [FIX #4] PV production degradation
│  │  └─ Annual degradation rate applied to sold/self_cons flows
│  ├─ [FIX #5] BESS replacement schedule
│  │  └─ Automatic calculation based on lifetime_years
│  └─ [IMPROVEMENT #4] Production degradation with correct purchased energy adjustment
│     └─ When production falls, purchased energy increases (demand constant)
│
└─ Output metrics:
   ├─ NPV: Net Present Value (€)
   ├─ pbp: Payback Period (years)
   ├─ capex: Total capital investment (€)
   ├─ Annual revenue components: r1, r2, r3, r4
   ├─ Annual cost components: c1, c2, c3, c4, c5
   ├─ Cashflow and cumulative cashflow over time
   └─ BESS replacement schedule

5. DETAILED FINANCIAL PARAMETERS EXTRACTION

ENERGY PRICING MODEL (carriers_and_costs in tutorial):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Base year (Year 1) prices:
├─ price_buy: 130 €/MWh
│  └─ Grid electricity purchase (residential prosumer tariff equivalent)
├─ price_sold: 104 €/MWh
│  └─ Grid electricity sale (feed-in tariff or peer-to-peer trading)
└─ decay: 0.02 (2% annual decline)

Price evolution formula:
├─ price_year_n = price_year_1 × (1 - decay)^(n-1)
├─ Example for price_buy:
│  ├─ Year 1: 130 €/MWh
│  ├─ Year 5: 130 × (1-0.02)^4 = 120 €/MWh
│  ├─ Year 10: 130 × (1-0.02)^9 = 107 €/MWh
│  └─ Year 20: 130 × (1-0.02)^19 = 87 €/MWh

DISCOUNT RATE & TAX IMPACT:
───────────────────────────

NPV calculation:
├─ int_rate: 0.03 (3% annual discount)
├─ formula: NPV = Σ CF_t / (1 + int_rate)^t
├─ Impact: Reduces future cashflows by ~3% per year
├─ Year 10 discounting: 1 / 1.03^10 = 0.744 (25.6% of nominal value)
├─ Year 20 discounting: 1 / 1.03^20 = 0.554 (44.6% loss in present value)
│
└─ Tax on sales:
   ├─ tax_rate: 0.20 (20% of revenue from grid sales)
   ├─ Applied to: r1 (revenue from selling electricity)
   ├─ Example: r1 = 10,000€ → c4 (tax cost) = 2,000€
   └─ Does NOT apply to self-consumption savings (r2)
